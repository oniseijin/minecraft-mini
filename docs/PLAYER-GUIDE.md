# Player guide

How to play minecraft-mini — for players. (The in-game **"How to play"** button on the title/pause menu mirrors this text; update both together.)

## Good to know

- **Why did my world suddenly change?** Someone started a mini-game — every connected player swaps into the match together, including your other browser tab (each tab is its own player with its own name). When the match ends, everyone returns to the sandbox. **Leave match** brings everyone back early.
- **Everything got slow** — usually two game tabs open at once (each runs a whole 3D world) or a stuck browser frame. Refresh the tab; it comes back fine.

## Sandbox — the open world

Build anything, break anything — the world saves by itself. **Punch = left-click** (on animals, zombies, or other players). Foxes, boars and giraffes wander the island — punch one and it runs away. At **night** zombies appear: they chase you and **shove** you around, but they can never hurt you. Punch back! Each hit staggers a zombie (5 punches defeats it) — but it won't flee, it turns on you, so hit and back off. They burn away at sunrise.

## BedWars 1v1

**Break her bed, then knock her into the void.** While your bed stands you respawn after 5 seconds; if your bed is gone, one death loses the match. The gaps are too wide to jump — you must **bridge** across.

Iron builds up over time. Stand on your island's shop pad (the glass marker) and press **B**: wool (4 iron) makes bridges, swords hit harder, golden apples heal (select, then right-click to eat). The middle island has **emeralds** for the best sword. At 10 minutes both beds break — sudden death!

## Block Party 1v1

The floor is colored wool. A color is announced — **stand on that color before the floor drops!** Wrong color = you fall. Every round is faster; first to **3 round wins**.

**Mystery Boxes:** from round 2, a spinning box appears on the floor. **Walk into it** to collect a random item — it appears in one of three slots above your hotbar. Press **1, 2 or 3** to use that slot, but only while the countdown is running, and **never in the last 2 seconds**. You can hold 3 items (only 1 teleporter). If you're losing, your boxes spawn closer to you!

- **Leap** — dash where you're facing
- **Speed** — faster for 5 seconds
- **Jump Boost** — huge jumps for 5 seconds (hop back onto the floor if you slip!)
- **Swapper** — instantly trade places with your opponent
- **Color Teleporter** — warp onto the safe color. Save it for when you're stranded!

**Color bombs:** a bomb lands on a *wrong* color and beeps faster and faster… then paints a 3×3 patch of the **safe color**. Race your opponent to claim that patch!

**The Zapper:** when *you* fall, you get **one** revenge shot — aim and click a floor tile to blow it up. If the survivor falls too, the round is a draw and replays!

## Murder Mystery co-op

You and your partner are **Detectives** in a big stone manor with **6 wandering guests** — and one of the guests is secretly the **murderer**! Watch closely and work out who it is before the **5:00** clock runs out.

**How do I know who did it?** Watch the tells: the murderer **never picks up coins**; innocent guests **run away from fresh gravestones** but the murderer doesn't; and the murderer sometimes **stops to stare at you**. In the final minute your HUD pulses **"The murderer is NEAR"** while it's within about 12 blocks — the guaranteed showdown!

**How do I stop the murderer?** Walk into coins (gold = 1, blue = 3, purple = 6) — every **10 coins** earns a zapper charge (you start with 1). Then **aim at the guest you think did it and LEFT-CLICK** the zapper (it reaches ~20 blocks). Zap the murderer and you both win! If the clock runs out first, the Detectives win anyway — the murderer ran out of time.

**What happens if I zap the wrong guest?** Your zapper **jams for 10 seconds** — but that guest is **proven innocent**, so even a wrong guess teaches you something!

**What does E do?** Stand next to a **gravestone** and press **E** to read it: who was got, and how long ago (once every 30 seconds).

**What if I fall?** The murderer needs **two hits** to get you (the first one shoves you back — run!). If you're got, you become a **ghost**: press `Space` ×2 or `F` to **fly around the manor and watch** your partner crack the case.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `W A S D` | move |
| Mouse | look around |
| Left click | break block / attack |
| Right click | place block / eat |
| `Space` | jump |
| `Space` ×2 or `F` | toggle flying |
| `Space` / `Shift` (flying) | ascend / descend |
| `Shift` or `Ctrl` or `W` ×2 | sprint |
| `1`–`9`, wheel | hotbar / held item slots |
| `B` | BedWars shop (near your island) |
| `1 2 3` (Block Party) | use mystery-box item |
| `Enter` or `T` | chat (Enter sends, Esc closes) |
| `Tab` (hold) | who's online |
| `[` / `]` | mouse sensitivity |
| `M` | mute |
| `Esc` | menu / close panels |
